Trunk · T0 · what MASH is, in one sentence

MASH is a single-process, shared-memory "semantic reality engine" — a TinyMUSH-descended text world of rooms, objects and agents held in one JSON-backed database, driven by a hardcoded-plus-softcoded command engine that routes every sensory or narrative gap through a Gemini "Loom" whose bracketed output is re-parsed as commands in the same syntax players and softcode use.

How this was reconstructed. Reverse-engineered July 21, 2026 from a read-only public clone by six parallel analysis agents reading mash_engine.py, ai_layer.py, database.py, app.py and README.md; every file:line is from the clone. The clone is shallow and carries no design records or roadmap, so the T5 tiers read as limits and frontiers visible in the code, not a stated roadmap.

Sibling to Trellis: both draw the same kernel/extensible-userspace line, compose prompts from state objects, and make their commands self-documenting. The difference is guards — MASH is a lower-stakes narrative engine where a model may freely author state and prompts; read through Trellis it is the un-guarded twin. Full analysis: SELF_DESCRIBING_SURFACES.md in the Trellis repo.
